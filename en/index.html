Services

Foundation & deep excavation works
01
Bored Piling

Cast-in-place bored piles for buildings and infrastructure across soft to hard soils, with verticality and concrete quality controlled on every pile.

Ø 400–1.200 mm · up to 30 m
02
Soldier Piling

Earth retaining walls for basement excavation and confined sites — bored piles set at measured spacing to suit retention requirements.

Deep excavation retention
03
Secant / Contiguous Piling

Continuous retaining walls formed by interlocking bored piles, for deep excavation with a high groundwater table.

Groundwater cut-off
04
Ground Anchors

Ground anchors stabilising retaining walls and slopes, proof-tested against design load.

Proof test per anchor
05
Dewatering

Groundwater control throughout construction, keeping excavations dry, stable, and safe to work in.

Groundwater table control
How we work

From soil data to integrity testing

01
Soil data review

We review CPT/borehole logs and shop drawings before settling on method and plant.

02
Rig mobilisation

Rig and crew are matched to site access, pile diameter, and design depth.

03
Drilling & concreting

Verticality, concrete slump, and pour volume are recorded pile by pile.

04
Reporting & integrity testing

Complete piling records and PIT/PDA testing to project specification.
